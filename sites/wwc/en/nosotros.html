Who we are

A logistics operator, not a middleman

WORLDWIDE COMMERCE GROUP LLC was born from a simple observation: thousands of people in Latin America wanted to buy products that didn't reach their country, and those who tried ran into declined cards, stores that didn't ship abroad and costs that only showed up at the end.

More than two decades later we're still solving that problem, but our reach has grown. Today we run our own warehouses in Miami and in three Chinese cities, have a regional office in Asunción, and serve both the person buying a pair of sneakers and the company importing pallets every month.

The difference our customers value most is the one that's least visible from the outside: when something goes wrong — a customs hold, a supplier who delivers badly, a package that doesn't show up — there's someone on the other end who has been through it hundreds of times.

  • Our own infrastructure, not rented space or outsourced handling
  • Spanish-speaking team by WhatsApp, phone and email
  • In-house technology, built by the people who run the logistics
  • Fixed price before dispatch, with itemized charges

How we work

Three commitments we keep

Price transparency

We show you an itemized quote before dispatch. If an unexpected cost comes up along the way, you approve it before it's incurred. No hidden fees: we say it because it's the most common complaint in this industry.

A person, not a form

Every account has a contact who knows its history. You won't have to explain your case again every time you write, or wait for a ticket system to assign you to someone different.

Verification before shipping

We inspect and photograph goods when they arrive at the warehouse. If something arrived damaged or incomplete, you find out while you can still file a claim, not after the package has crossed the continent.
