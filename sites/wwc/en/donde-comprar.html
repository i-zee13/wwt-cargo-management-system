Having trouble buying from or paying suppliers abroad?

No way to pay in China or the USA? We buy from and pay your supplier in the origin country for you, then ship the goods to your country.

Don't pause your business. We arrange direct payment to factories and stores in Asia in Chinese yuan or US dollars, consolidate your goods and prepare them for shipment to your country.

Assisted purchasing and supplier payment at origin

If you don't have international payment methods, enabled cards or RMB accounts, we pay your supplier in China, Asia or the USA under a commercial invoice and handle logistics through to your door.
