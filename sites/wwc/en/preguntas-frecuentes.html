Frequently asked questions

The questions we get most, answered straight. If yours is missing, write to us and we'll add it.

Home/Frequently asked questions
How does online shopping and shipping to Argentina work?
You buy online from any US store, send it to our Miami address, and we receive it, inspect it, repack it (if necessary) and ship it straight to your home in Argentina.
Can I buy from several stores and have them combined into a single shipment?
Yes. We can consolidate your packages from different stores and make a single shipment to save on costs.
What products can I buy and ship to Argentina?
You can ship clothing, electronics, supplements, tools, spare parts, toys, footwear and more. If you have questions about restricted products, feel free to ask us.
Is there a limit on value or weight?
There is no purchase limit. However, we help you optimize your shipments by weight, value and product type to avoid delays or unnecessary charges.
Do I have to pay additional taxes or fees?
No. Our service has no surprises. The price we quote includes everything: shipping, insurance, customs clearance and taxes.
How do I know my goods have reached their destination?
We share updates with photos and shipment tracking from the moment your goods arrive at our warehouse until you receive them at home.
Do you offer shipping insurance?
Yes. All our shipments include optional insurance to cover the declared value of your purchase.
How long does a shipment from Miami to Argentina take?
We offer Express (4 to 7 business days) and Standard (12 to 15 days). We also offer sea freight for large cargo (approx. 8 to 10 weeks).
Can I use your US address even if I haven't bought anything yet?
Of course. Sign up for free and we give you your personalized Miami address to use whenever you need it.
How do I track my shipment?
From your customer panel you can follow the status of your packages, see when they arrive at the warehouse, their preparation and their shipment to Argentina.
Do you offer complete moves from the USA to Argentina?
Yes. We take care of everything: packing, pickup, consolidation, customs procedures and delivery to your new home in Argentina.
Can I ship furniture and personal belongings?
Yes. You can ship all kinds of personal belongings, furniture, appliances and more. We assist you with the inventory and packing.
Can I also ship my car or motorcycle to Argentina?
Yes. We offer specialized vehicle transport, including title cancellation, container consolidation and assistance with registration.
How is the quote for my move calculated?
We provide a free, personalized quote based on volume, type of cargo and final destination in Argentina.
Can I store my belongings before shipping?
Yes. We include 3 months of free storage so you can organize your move in stages.
What documents do I need for an international move?
We guide you step by step. They include a certificate of residence, passport, packing list, vehicle titles, etc.
Which areas of Argentina do you cover?
We make door-to-door deliveries nationwide. You can also pick up from our local warehouses.
Will you help me with customs procedures in Argentina?
Yes. Our team handles all procedures, appointments, translations, duty payments and more.
Can I hire only some services and not the complete move?
Yes. Our services are modular. You can hire just the advice, the packing, the transport or the customs clearance, depending on what you need.
What makes Worldwide Commerce different from other companies?
We are a real team, with our own warehouses in Miami, China and Europe. Personalized attention, no middlemen and over 20 years of experience in international logistics.
